Meet the surgeon

Dr. Suraj Hindiskere

Lead Consultant, Musculoskeletal Oncology
SPARSH Hospitals, Bengaluru

MBBS, MS (Ortho), DNB (Ortho)

Dr. Suraj Hindiskere is an orthopaedic oncologist who treats benign and malignant bone and soft tissue tumours. He has over 10 years of practice and specialises in complex tumour removal and limb reconstruction surgeries.

Training

  • Fellowship in Orthopaedic Oncology, Seoul National University Hospital, South Korea
  • Clinical fellowship in Musculoskeletal Oncology, Istituto Ortopedico Rizzoli, Bologna, Italy
  • Fellowship in 3D printing in Orthopaedic Oncology, National Cancer Centre, Korea
  • UICC technical fellowship grant, 2019 and 2024

Areas of expertise

  • Limb salvage surgery with endoprosthetic (megaprosthesis) reconstruction
  • Biological reconstruction using recycled autograft and allograft
  • Patient-specific implants and 3D-printed implants
  • VR- and AR-assisted surgical planning
  • Paediatric reconstructions
  • Complex joint and limb reconstructions in adults
  • Navigation-guided surgeries
  • Pelvic tumour surgeries

Memberships

  • International Society of Limb Salvage (ISOLS), life member
  • Indian Musculoskeletal Oncology Society (IMSOS), life member
  • Indian Society of Oncology, life member
  • Indian Orthopaedic Association, life member

Languages

Kannada, Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam
