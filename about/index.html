About us

Why SarcomaHub exists

We started with a simple recognition: people facing sarcoma deserve clear information and a community that understands.

Our purpose

Sarcoma is sometimes called an orphan disease. It represents less than 2% of all newly diagnosed cancers, and limited awareness has left a large gap in knowledge. Patients and caregivers often feel lost, and parents of children with sarcoma carry the heaviest load as they navigate the emotional and practical side of this disease.

Beyond reliable information, people need a supportive community where they can share experiences and draw strength from each other. SarcomaHub aims to be that place, and to keep growing with the latest updates on sarcoma research, treatment and support.

We want to address concerns with real solutions, from discussing treatment options to offering emotional support. And we want to amplify the voices of the sarcoma community in advocating for policies that make quality care possible for everyone, whatever their income or where they live.

Our logo

The mandala, a sacred geometric pattern found across cultures, stands for unity and wholeness. In black and white it also echoes the pathways of a computer chip, honouring the place of technology in modern healthcare.

Inside, five concentric circles represent earth, fire, wind, water and space. At the centre is the yellow ribbon of sarcoma and bone cancer, a colour of healing and support. Together they stand for our commitment to cancer care and our belief that brighter tomorrows are within reach.
