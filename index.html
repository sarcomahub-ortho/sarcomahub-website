Know the warning signs

Most lumps are not cancer. But please see a doctor soon if you notice:

  • A lump that is getting bigger, or is larger than 5 cm
  • A lump that feels deep, under the muscle, whether or not it hurts
  • Bone pain that lasts, or wakes you at night
  • Swelling near a joint, or a bone that breaks after a minor injury

Ask for an MRI and a specialist opinion before any lump is removed. Sarcoma treated by a specialist team from the start gives the best chance of saving the limb.

Why SarcomaHub exists

No one should face a rare cancer in the dark

Sarcoma makes up less than 2% of newly diagnosed cancers. That rarity leaves many patients and families without accurate information, and parents of children with sarcoma often feel this the most. We want to close that gap and give the sarcoma community a place to share experiences and draw strength from each other.

Beyond reliable information, people need a supportive community. SarcomaHub aims to be that place, and to keep growing with the latest updates on sarcoma research, treatment and support.

We want to address concerns with real solutions, from discussing treatment options to offering emotional support. And we want to amplify the voices of the sarcoma community in advocating for policies that make quality care possible for everyone, whatever their income or where they live.

Our logo

A mandala with the sarcoma ribbon at its heart

The mandala, a sacred geometric pattern found across cultures, stands for unity and wholeness. In black and white it also echoes the pathways of a computer chip, honouring the place of technology in modern healthcare.

Inside, five concentric circles represent earth, fire, wind, water and space. At the centre is the yellow ribbon of sarcoma and bone cancer, a colour of healing and support. Together they stand for our commitment to cancer care and our belief that brighter tomorrows are within reach.
